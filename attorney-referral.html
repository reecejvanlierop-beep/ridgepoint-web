MVA Attorney Referrals

Refer a client injured in a motor vehicle incident. We'll contact your client to schedule and confirm with your office once their first visit is booked.

How It Works
  1. 1. Send the referral. Fill out the form with your client's contact and claim details.
  2. 2. We call your client. Our team reaches out to schedule the initial evaluation.
  3. 3. We confirm with you. Your office hears from us once the visit is booked, and we coordinate records and updates.
Billing

We bill the patient's own PIP coverage. We can't bill third-party auto insurance.

If PIP is exhausted, we can bill health insurance only for plans we're contracted with: Aetna, Regence, Premera, Cigna and First Choice. Otherwise the account is held on lien / LOP.
